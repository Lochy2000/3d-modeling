In Throw mode, click an object to throw it immediately. Drag in Orbit or Pan mode. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Alt + left-drag also orbits; right-drag, Shift-drag, or Shift-scroll pans. Arrow keys pan; + / − zoom. Home restores the original view. Knock other objects out of place to score. R resets the round; Esc pauses.

Click Enter room to capture your mouse. If mouse capture is unavailable, click objects directly and right-drag to look. Esc pauses. E picks up or drops what you’re holding (right-click also drops with mouse capture); scroll adjusts its distance. R returns you to the entrance. Reset objects starts the room over.
